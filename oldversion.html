Welcome to my website!

I am a third-year PhD student in Economics at the University of Houston. My research interests lie at the intersection of development economics, health economics, and environmental economics.

Before joining the doctoral program, I was a predoctoral fellow at the African School of Economics. I hold an M.A. in Economics from the University of Houston and a B.S. in Planning and Project Management (First Class Honours) from the École Nationale d'Économie Appliquée et de Management.
